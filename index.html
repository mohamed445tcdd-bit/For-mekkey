01 / The part I owe you

I’m sorry,
Mekkey.

I know that sometimes “I’m sorry” can sound too small for the way someone made you feel. And maybe I can’t undo a moment, a word, or a feeling that hurt you.

But I want you to know that I’m not saying this just because I miss the good moments. I’m saying it because I understand that I should have handled things better. You deserved more care from me, more patience, and a softer version of me when it mattered.

You are my Mekkey. My cat. And even when I mess things up, that has never been something I take lightly.

I’m not asking you to forget. I’m not trying to pressure you into forgiving me. I just wanted to leave the truth somewhere beautiful: I’m genuinely sorry, and you genuinely matter to me.

02 / If I could say only one thing

I never wanted to be
the reason your heart
felt a little heavier.

If there is a next conversation, I want to enter it with honesty. No ego. No pretending. Just me, trying to be better with the person who means so much to me.

03 / One last thing

If your heart ever
has room for a conversation...

I’ll be here. Not demanding an answer. Not rushing you. Just hoping that maybe, when you’re ready, we can talk.

To my Mekkey:

Whatever happens, I hope you never doubt how beautiful you are to me — not only in this picture, but in the way you exist in my memories, my thoughts, and my heart. I’m sorry, my cat. Truly.
